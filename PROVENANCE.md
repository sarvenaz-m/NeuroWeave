# Provenance and evidence

NeuroWeave is an independently implemented signal-analysis and cognitive-interaction research console, developed with AI assistance for code, documentation and original vector graphics.

## Signals and model

All packaged EEG examples and benchmark windows are generated by `research/neuro.py`. They are artificial signals with known frequency conditions, not recordings from participants. The generator, virtual-subject split and preprocessing are documented in the [dataset card](docs/DATASET_CARD.md).

The temporal CNN is a custom NumPy implementation with exported weights for a separately implemented JavaScript forward pass. It is not EEGNet or a reproduction of an external model's published performance. The training-fitted spectral baseline provides a transparent comparison. The [model card](docs/MODEL_CARD.md) describes assumptions, abstention and interpretation limits.

Imported signals are labelled user-supplied. Their source cannot be verified by schema validation, and model classification remains disabled for imports. Signal observations and task responses are independent records; the app does not acquire synchronized EEG during play.

## Interface and artwork

The interface uses original geometric response symbols and code-native SVG illustrations. `scripts/brand.py` produces editable brand assets. PNG versions are rendered derivatives; scientific figures are drawn from the benchmark data. Illustrations are not browser screenshots or acquired brain signals.

## Evaluation

Software checks cover numerical correctness, data handling and task state transitions. Synthetic benchmark performance does not establish accuracy on real EEG or clinical efficacy. The co-design and usability protocol describes proposed work; no participant study or clinical validation has been completed.
